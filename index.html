Our Purpose

You've done the maths in your head a hundred times.

The extra room changes everything — space for a growing family, a teenager who needs their own four walls, a home office that actually works. We built Fraternal because the existing options weren't good enough: $20,000 up front to buy, or $130 a week renting something that never becomes yours. There's a third way. Weekly payments that build toward ownership — and when four years are up, the cabin is yours outright, free and clear.

No — and this matters. Under your finance agreement, the cabin must remain freestanding personal property. It cannot be embedded in concrete, permanently bolted to a foundation, or affixed to land in a way that makes it a fixture. Once a cabin becomes part of the land, it legally stops being personal property — which compromises our PPSR registration and our ability to protect either party's interest in it.

A temporary base is fine: concrete pads, timber bearers, or adjustable piers all work well and keep the cabin removable. We'll advise on the best option for your site at the viewing. See our Placement Policy at the top of this section for the full details.

Yes — but you must give Fraternal written notice at least 14 days before the move. Email us the new address and we’ll confirm in writing. This is a condition of your finance agreement: Fraternal needs to know where our secured asset is at all times.

Relocations within Hawke’s Bay can generally be approved straightforwardly. Moving the cabin outside of Hawke’s Bay requires Fraternal’s prior written consent. All transport and relocation costs are your responsibility. Moving the cabin without notifying us may constitute a breach of your agreement.

No. Our cabins are consent-exempt sleepouts under Schedule 1 of the Building Act 2004 — which means no building consent, no council paperwork, and no delays. They're designed to sit on your property without any of the red tape that comes with permanent structures.

You apply through MTF Napier (our finance partner), and once approved, weekly payments are set up automatically — around $130 a week over 4 years. Every payment builds toward ownership. At the end of the term, the cabin is fully paid off and legally yours. No balloon payment, no catch.

You'll need a reasonably level area and access wide enough for a HIAB crane truck (typically a standard driveway width). A concrete pad isn't required — we can discuss the best base option for your site. We'll walk you through everything at your viewing so there are no surprises on delivery day.

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Who we are

Fraternal Ltd (“Fraternal”, “we”, “us”) operates from Hastings, Hawke’s Bay. We build and deliver rent-to-own cabins. This policy explains how we handle personal information in accordance with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

We collect information you provide directly: your name, email address, phone number, and any message you submit via our contact form or newsletter sign-up. We do not collect payment details — all finance is handled directly by MTF Napier.

Why we collect it

We use your information solely to respond to your enquiry, send you the updates you’ve requested, and communicate with you about your cabin order if you proceed. We do not use it for any other purpose.

Who we share it with

Your enquiry details are received via Web3Forms (a secure form delivery service) and stored in our business inbox. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except where required by law. If you choose to proceed with finance, you will provide your information directly to MTF Napier — that is a separate relationship governed by their own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We retain enquiry information for as long as is reasonably necessary to respond to and follow up on your enquiry. Newsletter subscribers are kept on our list until they unsubscribe.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020, you have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, and to ask us to correct it. You can also ask us to delete it. To make a request, email us at fraternalltd@gmail.com. We will respond within 20 working days.
